Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Riot Games Developer Policies & Discord Developer Terms

1. Introduction

Dummy Bot is a Discord bot designed to provide gaming, social, and moderation capabilities to independent Discord communities. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and retention, with special emphasis on our VALORANT player integration and Riot Sign On (RSO).

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Dummy Bot, we may collect and store the following data points:

  • Discord User ID: Used as the primary key to associate commands, linked game profiles, and preferences with your Discord account.
  • Linked Riot Account Data: When you connect your account via Riot Sign On or the /valorant link command, we store your Riot PUUID, Riot ID (Game Name and Tag Line), and preferred game region.
  • Guild Profile Visibility: A per-server consent flag indicating whether you have authorized Dummy Bot to display your rank in that specific Discord Guild.
  • Ranked Game Statistics: When requested in Discord, current competitive tier, Ranked Rating (RR), and act win counts retrieved strictly from official Riot Games APIs.
Strictly No Web Scraping: Dummy Bot strictly complies with Tracker Network and Riot Games terms. We do NOT scrape tracker.gg or any third-party websites. All player data originates from Riot Games' official APIs with verified player consent.

3. Privacy by Default (Guild Visibility)

We believe user privacy should be default, not optional:

  • Linking your Riot account does not expose your rank or stats across all Discord servers where the bot operates.
  • In each Discord Guild, your rank and stats remain hidden from other members until you explicitly run /valorant visibility enable in that server.
  • You can revoke your consent in any server at any time using /valorant visibility disable.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your stored data:

  • Self-Service Unlinking: Running /valorant unlink in Discord immediately and permanently deletes your linked Riot record from our database.
  • Guild Data Erasure: When Dummy Bot is removed from a Discord server, all server-specific records and visibility preferences for that server are permanently erased.
  • Complete Deletion Requests: You may request complete erasure of any records associated with your Discord user ID by contacting the Bot Owner or opening an issue on our repository.

5. Third-Party Services

This integration communicates with official Riot Games APIs to fetch competitive standings. Riot Games' handling of authentication and account data is governed by Riot Games' Privacy Notice.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or data deletion inquiries, please reach out via GitHub Issues on the Dummy-Bot Repository.

Dummy Bot is not endorsed by Riot Games, Inc.
