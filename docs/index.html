Dummy Bot Companion

Secure, policy-compliant Riot Sign On (RSO) authorization and data controls for your VALORANT profiles and Guild Leaderboards.

Riot Sign On (RSO) Authorization

Authorize Dummy Bot to query your official VALORANT rank, RR, and competitive tier. Dummy Bot strictly adheres to Riot Games Developer Policies and never scrapes third-party websites.

Privacy by Default Connecting your Riot account does NOT automatically reveal your stats in any Discord server. Your profile remains private until you explicitly grant consent in that server using /valorant visibility enable.

Redirects to https://auth.riotgames.com/authorize with scope openid.

Policy & Compliance

  • Riot Games Approved Uses official Riot Developer APIs with authorized player consent.
  • No Web Scraping Fully compliant with Tracker Network policies. Third-party scraping is prohibited.
  • Guild Visibility Controls Members retain full control over which Discord servers can see their stats.
  • Native Competitive Ordering Rankings strictly reflect official Riot Competitive Tiers and Ranked Rating.

Unlinking & Data Deletion

You can unlink your Riot account and remove your records at any time:

  • In Discord: Type /valorant unlink to delete your linked Riot record instantly.
  • Server Removal: When Dummy Bot leaves a Guild, all server-specific visibility settings are permanently removed.
  • Manual Deletion: Contact the Bot Owner to request complete deletion of all associated user data.
